# 2026-10-21: filming begins

The host started filming on a morning walk and shared a sample clip (`IMG_1873.mov`). He said he had
started "filming sideways". This entry records what that turned out to mean and what we did about it.

## What we found

- **"Sideways" is landscape, flipped.** The clip is 1920x1080 H.264 from an iPhone 15, 15.5 s, about 30 fps,
  stereo AAC at -20.5 LUFS integrated with a -2.7 dBFS true peak. The phone was held landscape and turned
  180 degrees, so the pixels are stored upside down with a rotate-180 flag. Players that honor the flag show
  it upright.
- **The pipeline is safe with it, as far as we could test.** `tools/make_proxy.py` and `tools/render_cuts.py`
  re-encode through `ffmpeg -vf scale=...`, and ffmpeg autorotates by default. We ran the same filter chain
  with libx264: the output is upright, carries no leftover rotation flag, and an upright frame comes out
  even when read with `-noautorotate`. We could not run NVENC (no GPU here), and nothing here has been run
  through Remotion. Look at a rendered frame on the first real cut.
- **The file carries GPS tags** and a timestamp. Raw footage is git-ignored here, which is right. Exports
  from the pipeline are re-encodes; check them before publishing anything that is not.
- **Episode 001 was not on `main`.** It exists only on draft PR #3 (`episodes/001-ham-technician/`, plus the
  `rda exam external` mode). Our first `rda status` said "no episodes yet" because the checkout had never
  seen that branch. The topic was in `johnnyautoseed/_work_efforts_/CHECKPOINT_2026_10_16_...md`.
