# 2026-10-19

## RDA joins one channel thesis

Worked in other repos today (Relaxation Station, thecoffeejesus); this entry records what changed for RDA.

The host decided the channel has one thesis, "What if you could build the world you want to live in?", a
practical, hopeful take on building the solarpunk future, and said: "go, fold everything in under the
thesis." RDA is now one of five formats under it (Finds, The Build, RDA, Relaxation Station, How I Make
This): the one where the host learns a skill the future needs against a real exam. See D17.

Nothing in the RDA formula, tools or rules changed. What changes is how a domain is picked: it should serve
the thesis. One example to check for episode 001: an entry-level solar installer exam such as NABCEP PV
Associate, sourced under `docs/exam-sourcing.md` (rights check first).

The channel plan, with its decisions, risks and first three episodes, lives in `ctavolazzi/thecoffeejesus`,
`series/build-the-world/`.
